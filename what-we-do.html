Think Again Creative was founded by three under-stimulated yet over-worked agency folk. Let's call them, the creative, the strategist and the generalist

These guys liked to sit in a local Newcastle pub and wax lyrical about the work they loved – from all spectrums of the creative and advertising world, the latest evidence and opinions from the great thinkers of this golden age of marketing disruption.

Then, they realised, everything they loved had one thing in common. It made them think again. It challenged, it discovered edges and pushed past them, it was brilliantly simple and it was grounded in the science of how we actually behave.

The friends asked, dare we think again about our careers? Dare we think again about who we work with and how we help make a contribution?

So here we are… to build authentic and credible brands that push boundaries, which challenge us to think again about a product, a service, or a category.

Do you have a boatload of experience, talent and are starting out like us? Do you need the creative inspiration to make your brand have real impact on your corner of the world? Then we are your guys.
